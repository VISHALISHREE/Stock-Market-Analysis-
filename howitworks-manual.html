User Manual & Guidelines

A detailed guide on how to use Trading Assistant safely and effectively.

What the Website Does

  • Provides a real-time dashboard for stock prices, trends, and performance indicators.
  • Interactive charts with customizable indicators and timeframes.
  • Automated alerts and notifications based on user-defined thresholds.
  • Customizable watchlists for your preferred assets.
  • Machine learning-powered predictive insights for smarter trading decisions.
  • Trade history analysis to review past performance and refine strategies.

What the Website Does NOT Do

  • It does not guarantee profits or investment success.
  • It does not execute trades automatically — user confirmation is required.
  • It is not a substitute for professional financial advice or consultation.
  • It cannot access private brokerage accounts without authorization.
  • It does not provide legal, tax, or compliance advice.

User Cautions & Recommendations

  • Always verify trade signals before taking action; consider news and market volatility.
  • Set stop-loss and risk management parameters carefully.
  • Do not rely solely on predictive analytics — combine with your own research.
  • Keep credentials secure and never share account details.
  • Monitor watchlists and alerts regularly to avoid missing critical updates.
  • Avoid over-trading or emotional decisions during volatile periods.

Best Practices

  • Customize your dashboard for clarity and focus on relevant assets.
  • Use charts to compare trends across multiple timeframes.
  • Check trade history often to refine strategies and learn from past trades.
  • Combine alerts with personal analysis for informed decisions.
  • Keep the interface uncluttered; use watchlists and filters strategically.
  • Take breaks during long sessions to prevent fatigue and mistakes.

Technical Notes & Limitations

  • Real-time data may have minor delays depending on market feed sources.
  • Machine learning insights rely on historical data; always check current conditions.
  • Platform performs best on modern browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
  • Large watchlists may reduce performance on older devices.
  • Internet connection is required for real-time features; offline mode is not supported.

Additional Tips

  • Update watchlists regularly to remove inactive assets.
  • Use alerts for both opportunities and potential risks.
  • Experiment with chart indicators to find the setup that works best for you.
  • Engage consistently with the platform — frequent monitoring improves decisions.
  • Document lessons learned to build your personal trading playbook.
